## Executive purpose
Preserve the final-accepted CDX 1.0 constitutional, runtime and estate governance foundation while establishing a substantive, evidence-grounded legal and ethical conduct-assurance capability. CDX 2.0 shall strengthen external-law applicability, interpretation, qualified review and human-rights sensitivity; integrate the existing HCC moral doctrine; construct an internal, command-invoked Legal Audit of Conduct for Excellence (LACE); verify actual effects; and obtain a separately governed release decision.

CDX governs. LACE examines. HCC contributes the moral and human-consequence doctrine originating within Pius. External applicable law and fundamental rights remain superior to internal rules. LACE does not become a separate system, anchor or independent authority. Building a legal-specialist agent is outside this programme.

5. Draft CDX–HCC relationship PR #71 is not presumed merged. Current HCC-S1-V1.0 is source-pinned; no doctrinal revision occurs before an accepted Pius 6 source and a separately authorized HCC review.

9. Source-controlled changes are made on branches via pull requests, never direct mutation of protected main.
