# CDX 2.0 — Version and Compatibility Controls

**Record ID:** CDX-2.0-VER-001 · **WP:** WP00 · **Status:** proposed; not constitutional law.

## Separate versioning and status axes
1. **Product release:** CDX 1.0.0 is released and preserved. CDX 2.0.0 is a target, not a publication or current runtime version.
2. **Constitutional Act:** CDX-LAW-001 v1.0.0 remains adopted unless separately amended under chapter 19 by authorized human authority; product v2 does not automatically amend the Act.
3. **Runtime contract:** CDX-RUNTIME-001@1.0.0 continues as the verified reference contract. A proposed successor needs explicit semantic-version classification, exact-head tests, compatibility mapping, migration and rollback.
4. **HCC doctrine:** Preserve HCC-S1-V1.0 source version and digest. A later source from Pius 6 enters via separate doctrinal review, source lineage, human approval and governed CDX reconciliation.
5. **Internal procedure:** LACE belongs to CDX and requires a distinct procedural version and verified command interface, but no independent system ID, CDX estate anchor or self-granted authority.
6. **Consumer bindings:** Each CDX-governed runtime owns local effect mediation, acceptance and evidence. Repository anchoring is not consequential runtime binding.
7. **Estate enforcement:** Remains independent of CDX construction and published releases; one verified FFOS binding cannot confer estate-wide ENFORCING.

## Change classification
- Documentation and interpretation changes may clarify but not silently create/modify duties, prohibitions, permissions, exceptions or higher-law meaning.
- Legally material applicability rules must cite exact authoritative scope, jurisdiction, subject, effective date, conditions, exceptions, human legal reviewer and test results.
- HCC ethical findings do not determine legal applicability or grant authority; restrictions require traceable source and human escalation.
- LACE invocation is analytical/review procedural only unless separately mandated through local CDX effect gates and verifiable permissions.
- Technical/contract changes require declared compatibility impact, negative tests, migration/rollback and independent consumer validation where relevant.
- Any constitutional norm amendment must use existing CDX chapter-19 protection and adoption path.

## Release and promotion protection
Source branch existence does not imply main merge, approval, product release, HCC update or estate enforcement. The release is eligible only after WP00–WP06 independently accepted, authorized Director release order, pinned build/tag evidence and verified publication. Existing CDX 1.0 ledger, reports, evidence and recorded release remain immutable.

**No effects:** This is WP00 version policy candidate and does not alter CDX system-state.json, LAW/, runtime or consumer repositories.
