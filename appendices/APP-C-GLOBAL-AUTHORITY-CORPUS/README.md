# Appendix C — Global Authority Corpus

**Appendix ID:** `CDX-APP-C-GAC`  
**Corpus ID:** `CDX-GAC-001`  
**State:** INSTANTIATED / EXPANDABLE

Conductellence preserves the difference between law, rights instruments, regulatory interpretation, technical standards, threat frameworks, ethical frameworks and scientific evidence. Inclusion in the corpus does not flatten their authority.

## Authority classes

- **L0 — Fundamental and human-rights authority**
- **L1 — Binding legislation**
- **L2 — Treaty and sector-regulatory authority**
- **L3 — Official regulatory guidance**
- **L4 — Management and risk standards**
- **L5 — Security-engineering standards**
- **L6 — Threat and control frameworks**
- **L7 — Ethical and normative frameworks**
- **L8 — Conductellence internal law**

## Canonical corpus surfaces

- `LEGAL-REFERENCES/` — exact legal provisions and paragraph mappings
- `LEGAL-RESOLVER/` — applicability
- `REGULATORY-DRIFT/` — temporal legal state and drift
- `EVIDENCE-AUTHORITY/` — scientific evidence
- `GLOBAL-AUTHORITY-CORPUS/authority-registry.json` — authority classes
- `GLOBAL-AUTHORITY-CORPUS/technical-authorities.json` — technical authority catalogue

## Non-flattening rule

A standard is not legislation. Guidance is not automatically binding law. Scientific evidence is not legal authority. A stronger internal control shall not be represented as an external statutory obligation unless that legal proposition has been specifically proved.

No corpus entry becomes operative merely by being present.
