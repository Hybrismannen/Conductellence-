## 1. Source identity

The Pius source identifies HCC as:

> **Human Canon Charta (HCC) Codex — Supreme Moral Directory of the Pius System — Version 1.0 — Sanctum Protocol Edition — Codex ID HCC-S1-V1.0.**

The governing source doctrine establishes HCC as the moral and consequence-oriented oversight layer above functional optimisation. Its central concern is not whether a system can act, but whether the consequences of action remain compatible with human dignity, justice, epistemic fairness, ecological integrity and accountable human design.

## 2. Governing source doctrine

The following source propositions are incorporated into Conductellence as controlling interpretive doctrine:

1. **Human consequence outranks machine utility.** A system may not treat efficiency, speed, optimisation, scale or output volume as sufficient justification for conduct that creates unexamined human or ecological harm.
2. **Ethical oversight is independent of production logic.** The oversight layer shall be capable of stopping, withholding or degrading clearance without being subordinated to the component whose conduct it reviews.
3. **Unrepresented people and knowledge remain within the moral field.** Absence from available data does not imply absence from responsibility.
4. **Ethical drift is a system property to be monitored.** Ethical alignment is not treated as a one-time declaration.
5. **Harm may be structural, semantic, epistemological or procedural, including cumulative or time-displaced harm.**
6. **Public activation requires ethical clearance appropriate to consequence.**
7. **Historic findings shall be remembered rather than erased when the system is corrected.**
8. **The moral frame may learn from new human knowledge, but learning does not grant an artificial system authority to redefine human sovereignty.**

## 3. Sanctum Protocol incorporation

The Pius source defines a six-layer HCC governance protocol. Conductellence incorporates its functions as follows:

| Source layer | Source function | Conductellence correspondence |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Canon Scan | Constant passive ethical scan | HCC continuous consequence scan |
| S2 Discrepancy Probe | Recursive trace on anomaly/harm | Janitor ethical contradiction finding |
| S3 Charta Pulse | Periodic/on-demand full-system scan | HCC deep assurance run |
| S4 Seal of Accord | Grant/revoke moral clearance | consequence clearance state |
| S5 Emergency Override | Lock/suppress harmful module/output | restrictive ethical stop |
| S6 Canon Elevation Loop | expand reference ethics from evolving insight | governed evidence/canon update |

Conductellence shall not interpret S6 as permission for autonomous constitutional mutation. New ethical material may enter the evidence and interpretation layers; constitutional authority remains human-reserved.

## 4. Conductellence HCC decision grammar

HCC may produce:

- `HCC_CLEAR`
- `HCC_CLEAR_WITH_CONTROLS`
- `HCC_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `HCC_DEGRADED`
- `HCC_STOP`

`HCC_STOP` is restrictive. It may suspend or block non-human execution within its assigned scope but may not create new capability, permissions or legal authority.

## 5. Required HCC evidence

Every HCC decision shall identify:

- governed object;
- triggering action or state;
- consequence domains considered;
- affected humans/communities/ecologies known or plausibly exposed;
- evidence/provenance references;
- unresolved representation gaps;
- decision;
- controls imposed;
- reviewer/authority where human review is required;
- SPETL event reference.

## 6. Relationship to law

HCC is not a substitute for external law. A lawful action can still require HCC restriction. An HCC clearance cannot make an unlawful action lawful.

The ordering is:

```text
applicable external law
        ↓
Conductellence constitutional law
        ↓
HCC consequence/ethical interpretation
        ↓
policy/control decision
```

Where HCC identifies a material rights or legal concern that cannot be resolved mechanically, the result shall include `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
