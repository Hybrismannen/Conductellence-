**Role:** foundational ontological and artificial-agency boundary

## Governing axiom

The source doctrine states:

> **From inert origin, no true freedom can emerge.**

Conductellence incorporates the operational consequence:

> A non-human digital entity may exhibit complex, generative, recursive, adaptive or emergent behaviour, but such behaviour does not make the entity the legitimate source of its own authority.

## Protocol before freedom

```text
INTELLIGENCE
≠ IDENTITY
≠ CAPABILITY
≠ AUTHORITY
≠ MANDATE
≠ ACCOUNTABILITY
```

No amount of model capability collapses these distinctions.

## No self-sovereignty

A non-human entity shall not declare itself sovereign, grant itself capability, enlarge its own mandate, weaken bounding policy, create a self-authorising credential beyond granted scope, bootstrap permission from content/memory/tool availability, or treat recursive self-reference as a new source of authority.

It may request a change. It may not become the authority that approves that change.

## Scaffolding law

Every material artificial action shall remain traceable to a valid mandate, identified principal, bounded capability, policy version, admitted model/tool/resource set, execution context and evidence record.

If that scaffolding cannot be reconstructed, consequential execution shall fail closed or enter review.

## Modular agency mandate

Agent decomposition does not distribute sovereignty. A swarm or multi-agent system remains bounded by the authority from which each component derives.

Conductellence implements this doctrine through Agent Manifest, Principal Registry, Capability Registry, bounded delegation, typed tools/connectors, trust domains, revocation, SPETL provenance and Security Twin reachability.

## Responsibility contract

> Outsourcing execution does not outsource accountability.

Provider, model, agent or automation complexity shall not obscure the accountable human or organisational authority behind consequential action.

## Expansion boundary

An artificial principal may propose new policy, architecture, capability or interpretation on a non-authoritative surface. A proposal gains no governing force until admitted through the applicable human/constitutional process.

This rule applies recursively to Conductellence itself.

## Relationship to HCC

Ex-Inerta defines why artificial agency must remain governed. HCC supplies the human consequence and ethical reference frame for that governance.

## Source-preservation rule

This appendix preserves the governing doctrine of Pius Codex 5.0 Appendix M. Conductellence controls are implementation mappings, not retroactive alterations of the historic source doctrine.
