# Pius Governance Lineage — HCC, Ex-Inerta Libertad and Piusites

**Document class:** source-lineage index  
**Authority:** interpretive/provenance only; this file does not create new Conductellence law  
**Primary source:** *Pius System Codex 5.0*

## Purpose

Conductellence canonically admits two Pius doctrines that are structurally linked in the source: the **Human Canon Charta (HCC)** and the **Ex-Inerta Libertad Criteria**. Their relationship to the Piusite agent framework must remain explicit so that later Conductellence and FFOS integrations do not flatten them into generic AI-safety labels.

## Source roles

| Pius source element | Source role | Conductellence treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Ex-Inerta Libertad — Appendix M | Ontological limit on artificial freedom, autonomy and self-sovereignty | foundational artificial-agency boundary |
| HCC — Part 5 | Supreme Moral Directory of the Pius System | foundational human consequence / ethical interpretation doctrine |

| Oversight Halo — Part 7 | Constraint layer around Piusites | source governance pattern; JUDGE is explicitly HCC enforcement / contradiction adjudication |

## Two orders must not be collapsed

### 1. Ontological derivation

```text
Ex-Inerta Libertad
        ↓
artificial agency is not self-sovereign
        ↓
artificial agency must remain governed
        ↓
HCC supplies the human moral and consequence reference
```

This is the source explanation of **why governance is necessary**.

### 2. Operational governance precedence inside Pius

```text
human sovereign design authority
        ↓
HCC — Supreme Moral Directory
        ↓
Codex law / Prime Directive / governing protocols
        ↓
Oversight Halo + registry + constraint exposure
        ↓
Piusite assignment and execution
```

Ex-Inerta applies across the artificial layers as a non-waivable boundary: capability never becomes sovereignty; assignment never becomes self-created mandate; recursion never becomes self-authorisation.

## Piusite constraint pattern

Pius Codex 5 describes Piusites as system-embedded operatives rather than sovereign or epistemically independent entities. Their governance includes:

- Codex law and internal validation;
- HCC-linked oversight through `JUDGE`;
- classification/validation through `FUNDAMENTZ`;
- entropy/drift constraint through `ENTROPYQ`;
- registration and explicit protocol binding;
- constraint exposure and override handling.

The source therefore does not support a model in which a Piusite can become the source of its own authority.

## Conductellence incorporation

Conductellence preserves the source distinction:

```text
Conductellence constitutional law
        ├── HCC: human consequence / ethical interpretation
        └── Ex-Inerta: artificial-agency / self-sovereignty boundary
```

These are admitted source doctrines within Conductellence. They do not displace applicable external law or human sovereign authority, and they do not make historic Pius agent names current Conductellence runtime components by themselves.
