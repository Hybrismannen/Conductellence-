# Legal Reference Policy

**Canonical policy:** `CDX-LEGAL-REF-001`  
**Applies to:** `CDX-LAW-001`

## Rule

Every normative prose paragraph in the Conductellence Act shall carry at least one clickable reference to an official text of a real legal instrument.

A legal link does **not** by itself establish that the cited instrument applies to every Conductellence-governed system. Applicability is determined separately under 2 kap. of the Act.

## Anchor classes

- **TEXTUAL** — the cited legal text contains a materially similar principle, duty, prohibition, safeguard or governance requirement.
- **DERIVED** — the Conductellence paragraph operationalises or generalises a legal requirement into a broader internal rule.
- **CONTEXTUAL** — the legal instrument establishes a relevant legal context, right or governance concern, but does not itself prescribe the exact Conductellence mechanism.

No anchor class may be used to imply a stronger legal obligation than the source actually supports.

## Primary-source rule

Mandatory anchors in the Act shall point to primary or official legal sources wherever available: EUR-Lex, Sveriges riksdag/Svensk författningssamling, official treaty repositories, or equivalent official national sources.

Standards, technical specifications, academic literature and regulatory guidance remain valuable evidentiary sources, but **do not satisfy the legal-anchor requirement**. They shall be stored in the Global Authority Corpus and Interpretation Guide as separate source classes.

## Article precision

The Interpretation Guide shall progressively refine whole-instrument citations into article/section-level pinpoints. The Act may cite the official instrument as a whole during bootstrap, provided the citation label identifies the relevant article(s) or legal subject matter.

## Non-applicability

Where a cited law is used only as a comparative legal anchor and is not applicable to the system, the citation remains valid as provenance for Conductellence's internal rule but shall not appear in the Legal Resolver as an applicable obligation.

## Review

Every Act amendment shall fail constitutional audit if it introduces a normative prose paragraph without a legal anchor.
