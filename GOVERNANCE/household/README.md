# Conductellence Household Family — Legal Integrity Programme

**Programme:** `CDX-PROG-LEGAL-BD-001`  
**Human clearance:** `CDX-CLR-LEGAL-BD-001`  
**Source operating doctrine:** FFOS Household Family pattern, adapted to Conductellence jurisdiction.

Conductellence uses the Household Family for process control without transferring constitutional or legal judgment to the Household roles.

## Director

Director owns **progression and acceptance**, not legal truth.

Director may:

- dispatch a dependency-ready work unit under the active human clearance;
- verify declared acceptance criteria against committed state;
- block progression;
- record PASS only after the required evidence exists.

Director may not:

- manufacture human/legal acceptance;
- convert an unresolved legal question into PASS;
- treat an ephemeral repair as committed evidence;
- amend Conductellence constitutional law.

## Janitor

Janitor owns surveillance of:

- paragraph/provision identity coherence;
- primary-source provenance;
- citation syntax and locator integrity;
- semantic-proof state contradictions;
- applicability-state contradictions;
- temporal legal-state drift;
- CI/audit health;
- orphaned/stale registry records;
- security and provenance tripwires.

Janitor reports findings using:

`INFO → HYGIENE → DRIFT → CONTRADICTION → CONTROL_DEFECT → SECURITY_STOP → CONSTITUTIONAL_STOP`

For the B–D programme, legal uncertainty may additionally produce `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Handyman

Handyman may execute only registered deterministic J1 repairs.

A repair qualifies as J1 only where the correct result follows mechanically from already accepted authoritative state.

Examples:

- regenerate a queue from the canonical paragraph map;
- correct an ID that deterministically differs from the canonical registry;
- regenerate a derived index;
- replace a copied locator with the exact already-verified locator.

Handyman may not determine:

- what a law means;
- whether a law applies;
- whether two propositions are semantically equivalent;
- whether an exception or exemption applies;
- whether an external amendment changes Conductellence doctrine.

Those are J2.

## Household controller

The controller sequences Director, Janitor and Handyman. It acquires no additional authority and cannot combine their separated powers into a super-role.

## Phase continuity

`CDX-CLR-LEGAL-BD-001` authorizes continuous progression through B → C → D. A new conversational proceed prompt is not required at ordinary boundaries.

Progression stops for:

- `SECURITY_STOP`;
- `CONSTITUTIONAL_STOP`;
- unresolved `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- disputed semantic equivalence;
- disputed legal applicability;
- primary-source conflict;
- a required constitutional amendment;
- explicit human hold or revocation.
